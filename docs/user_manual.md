## 1. Welcome to Recollect

Recollect is an assistive routine support and monitoring platform designed to bring peace of mind to seniors living with early-stage cognitive changes, their families, and their healthcare providers.

### The "Calm Over Clever" Design Philosophy
* **For Seniors:** We avoid cluttered screens, confusing menus, tiny fonts, and loud negative alerts. Seniors interact with gentle, single-decision cards and reassuring audio.
* **For Family Caregivers:** Real-time visibility into tablet interactions, adherence history, customizable alert escalations, and direct communication with healthcare workers.
* **For Doctors & Health Workers:** Evidence-based, explainable behavioral signals without false alarms or unverified AI diagnoses.

## 3. Senior Patient Space (Bedside Tablet Guide)

The Senior Patient Space is optimized for 10-inch tablets placed on a bedside table, desk, or kitchen counter.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| [Recollect] (Senior Space)            [🔊 Listen]  [Aa Text Size]  [🚨 Help]|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                                         |
|   Good Morning, Eleanor!                                                |
|   Monday, September 7, 2026 • Morning Sunshine, 24°C                    |
|                                                                         |
|   +-----------------------------------------------------------------+   |
|   | 💊 Current Routine: Morning Medicine (9:00 AM)                  |   |
|   | 1 Yellow Oval V 42 (Lisinopril 10mg) with 1 Full Glass of Water |   |
|   |                                                                 |   |
|   |               [ ✓  I took my medicine ]                         |   |
|   +-----------------------------------------------------------------+   |
|                                                                         |
|   +-------------------+  +-------------------+  +-------------------+   |
|   | 🌿 Mind Games     |  | 😊 How I'm Feeling|  | 📞 Call Sarah     |   |
|   +-------------------+  +-------------------+  +-------------------+   |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 3.1 Understanding the Orientation Screen
* **Dynamic Greeting:** Greets Eleanor warmly based on the actual time of day:
  * *Morning (5:00 AM – 11:59 AM):* **"Good Morning, Eleanor!"**
  * *Afternoon (12:00 PM – 4:59 PM):* **"Good Afternoon, Eleanor!"**
  * *Evening (5:00 PM – 8:59 PM):* **"Good Evening, Eleanor!"**
  * *Night (9:00 PM – 4:59 AM):* **"Restful Evening, Eleanor!"**
* **Calendar Date:** Clearly displays the current day of the week, month, and year in the selected language.
* **Weather Bar:** Shows pleasant current conditions (e.g., *"Morning Sunshine • 24° Mild"*).

### 3.2 Spoken Narration ("Listen to Your Day")
* Tap the **"🔊 Listen to your day"** button at any time.
* The tablet reads aloud a calm briefing in a warm, unhurried voice explaining the date, the current time, and what routine is scheduled next.
* If your device does not have speech synthesis installed or is muted, a gentle pentatonic melodic chime plays instead.

### 3.3 Taking Scheduled Medications & Routines
* When it is time for medicine, the large card displays:
  * The name of the routine (e.g., *Morning Blood Pressure Pill*).
  * The pill's exact visual description: *"Yellow Oval V 42 (Lisinopril 10mg)"*.
  * Water instructions: *"1 Full Glass of fresh cool water"*.
* **Taking the Medicine:** Tap the green **"✓ I took my medicine"** button.
* **Confirmation:** The card immediately updates with a green confirmation checkmark (*"Completed at 9:02 AM • Wonderful job, Eleanor!"*), plays audio feedback, and notifies Sarah's Caregiver dashboard.

### 3.4 Full-Screen Attention Reminder Takeover
For critical routines, a full-screen card appears over the screen:
* Displays a clear picture of the pill and a glass of water.
* Gives you three simple choices:
  1. **"✓ Done"**: Tap if you have taken your medicine.
  2. **"⏰ Remind me in 10 minutes"**: Snoozes the reminder gently.
  3. **"🤝 Need help from Sarah"**: Sends a notification to Sarah asking her to come assist.

### 3.5 Playing Stress-Free Cognitive Mind Games (Full-Screen Experience)
Tap **"🌸 Play a Game"** to open the Mind Games Hub, then choose any of the 4 cognitive activities. Games launch immediately into **Full-Screen Immersion** (`100vw` × `100vh`), providing large touch targets and zero distractions:

* **Zero Timers, Ever:** Take all the time you need. There are no countdown clocks, pressure, or penalties.
* **Patient-Side Garden Score Tracker:**
  * View your daily **Garden Stars (⭐)** in Eleanor's top orientation bar and inside the in-game header bar.
  * Your garden advances from **Sprout (🌱)** to **Budding Green (🌿)**, **Blooming Daisy (🌼)**, and **Sunflower Champion (🌻)** as you complete gentle games.
* **Playing the 4 Distinct Games:**
  1. **🌸 Memory Game (Photo Match):**
     * Switch between **Family Photos** (Lily, Buddy, Garden Rose, Tea) and **Northeast Cultural Pack** (Assam Tea, Bihu Drum, Brahmaputra Sunset, Rhino).
     * Touch two cards to peek. Find all matching pairs.
  2. **🔍 Attention Game (Garden Flower Focus):**
     * Look at the target flower spotlight at the top (e.g., *"Spot and tap all 3 blooming Sunflowers 🌻"*).
     * Spot and tap the matching flowers in your garden bed. Target flowers light up with blooming gold halos!
  3. **🗣️ Language Game (Word & Object Recall):**
     * Read or listen to the question (e.g., *"What do we use to drink our warm morning tea?"*).
     * Tap the matching everyday object card (e.g., 🫖 Teacup & Teapot) across 3 gentle rounds.
  4. **🧩 Problem Solving Game (Daily Routine Steps):**
     * Look at the 4 routine slots at the top (Steps 1 to 4).
     * Tap the shuffled routine cards below in the order you do them (e.g., 🌅 Wake up -> 🥤 Drink water -> 💊 Take medicine -> 🥣 Breakfast).
* **In-Game Assistance & Controls:**
  * **"💡 Give Me a Gentle Hint":** Provides a gentle hint tailored to the active game without penalty.
  * **"🔊 Read Instructions":** Reads spoken rules aloud in your chosen regional language.
  * **"✕ Pause & Rest":** Touch anytime in the top corner or bottom bar to pause and return safely to your home screen.
* **Warm Celebration Screen:**
  * Celebrates your achievement with awarded Garden Stars (e.g., `⭐ +5 Garden Stars Bloomed!`) and session points, followed by a warm greeting before returning to your garden home.

### 3.6 Checking In Your Daily Mood
Tap **"😊 How I am Feeling Today"**:
* Tap any of the four friendly faces:
  * ☀️ **Joyful**
  * 🌸 **Peaceful**
  * 🛋️ **Tired**
  * 🤝 **Need Support**
* If Sarah is helping you, she can check the *"Caregiver-assisted"* box.
* Tap **"Done"** to save your mood.

### 3.7 Calling Family Members
* Tap **"📞 Call Sarah"** on the home screen.
* A friendly calling screen opens with a ringing pulse and an elapsed timer.
* When finished speaking, tap the red **"🔴 End Call"** button.

### 3.8 Urgent Care & Emergency Help ("Call Help")
* If you feel unwell, dizzy, or need immediate assistance, tap the red **"🚨 Call Help"** button in the top corner.
* The screen immediately alerts Sarah, queues a backup alert to Priya, and provides a direct button to call the National Emergency Helpline (**112**).

### 3.9 Adjusting Text Size Directly from the Tablet
* Tap the **"Aa Text Size"** button on the top orientation bar to cycle through:
  * **Normal (20pt)**
  * **Large (24pt / +20%)**
  * **Extra Large (28pt / +40%)**

## 4. Family Caregiver Portal Guide (Sarah's Hub)

The Family Caregiver Portal provides complete visibility into Eleanor's daily routine, behavioral trends, and medical coordination.

### 4.1 Accessing the Portal (PIN `1234`)
* On the Space Selection screen, select **Family Caregiver**.
* Enter PIN `1234` and click **Log In as Caregiver**.

### 4.2 Hero Bar & Live Tablet Pairing Status
* **Patient Live Pill:** Shows Eleanor's pairing status (*"Active in Living Room • Bedside Tablet Paired"*).
* **Live Hub Sync Badge:** Displays the exact time of the last synchronization with the bedside tablet (e.g., *"Live Hub Sync: 9:02 AM"*).
* **Quick Action Buttons:**
  * **"➕ Add Reminder"**: Authors a new routine item.
  * **"📞 Call Eleanor"**: Launches a voice call to Eleanor's tablet.
  * **"🚨 Emergency Quick Call"**: One-tap emergency escalation.

### 4.3 Handling Overdue Medication Alerts
When a high-priority routine is overdue:
* A prominent yellow-and-amber **Overdue Routine Alert Banner** appears at the top of your dashboard.
* It displays the overdue duration (e.g., *"35m Overdue"*) and the exact medication name.
* Three quick actions are available:
  1. **"Call Eleanor's Tablet"**: Speaks to Eleanor directly through her tablet.
  2. **"Mark Taken by Caregiver"**: Confirms that you administered the pill in person, immediately clearing the alert.
  3. **"Test SMS/USSD Fallback"**: Verifies SMS delivery in case of internet failure.

### 4.4 Managing Today's Visual Schedule Checklist (Tab 1)
* Click **"🗓️ Today's Schedule"** to view Eleanor's complete routine.
* Each routine card shows its scheduled time, category icon, and status badge (*Upcoming*, *Due Now*, or *Done*).
* **Interactive Actions:**
  * Click **"✓ Done"** to manually mark any task as completed.
  * Click **"↺ Undo"** to revert an accidental check-off.
  * Click **"✕"** to remove a custom task you created.
  * Click **"🔄 Refresh"** to pull the latest sync state from local storage.

### 4.5 Adding Custom Routine Tasks
1. Click the **"➕ Add Task"** button in the schedule header (or top hero bar).
2. Enter the routine details in the modal:
   * **Routine / Medication Name:** (e.g., *"Afternoon Chamomile Tea & Hydration"*).
   * **Category:** Medication, Hydration, Meal, or Activity/Walk.
   * **Scheduled Time:** Select time (e.g., `15:30`).
   * **Senior Instructions:** Clear, simple instructions displayed to Eleanor (e.g., *"Drink 1 full cup of warm tea"*).
   * **Alert Priority:** Choose *Normal* or *Critical (5-minute escalation alert)*.
3. Click **"Save & Sync to Device"**. The task immediately appears on both your checklist and Eleanor's tablet.

### 4.6 Analyzing Longitudinal Trends (Tab 2)
Click **"📈 Trends & Analytics"** to inspect Eleanor's cognitive and adherence trajectory:
* **Timeframe Selectors:** Click **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, or **Quarterly**.
* **Interactive SVG Graph:**
  * 🟢 **Green Line:** Routine Adherence Rate (tracks medication compliance).
  * 🔵 **Blue Dashed Line:** Composite Cognitive Score (tracks engagement and focus).
* Hover over data points to see specific dates and performance percentages.

### 4.7 Inspecting Notable Observations & Evidence
* Below the trend chart, review **Notable Activity Observations**.
* Click the accordion banner (e.g., *"⚠️ Routine Latency: Morning pill delay observed (+18 mins)"*) to expand:
  * **Underlying Raw Evidence:** Shows exact millisecond delays and logged activity context.
  * **Clinical Recommendation:** Practical advice for whether action is warranted.

### 4.8 Keeping the Family Memory Journal (Tab 3)
Click **"📝 Family Memory Journal"**:
1. Type your observation into the text box (e.g., *"Eleanor recognized old photo albums with great enthusiasm today"*).
2. Choose an emotional tag:
   * ✨ *Cognitive Spark*
   * 🌿 *Calm & Engaged*
   * 🛋️ *A Bit Tired*
   * ❓ *Mild Confusion*
3. Click **"💾 Save Note"**.
4. All saved entries are timestamped, preserved in Eleanor's longitudinal history, and automatically included in Dr. Thorne's weekly clinical report.

### 4.9 Managing the Care Circle & Multi-Author Shift Logs (Tab 4)
Click **"👥 Care Team & Messages"**:
* **Care Circle Cards:** View contact details for Sarah (Primary), Priya (Secondary), Dr. Thorne (Neurologist), and Anita Roy (ASHA Lead).
* **Add Member:** Click **"➕ Add Member"** to register a new family helper or nurse.
* **Shared Activity & Hand-off Log:**
  1. Select the author: *Sarah Vance (Primary)*, *Priya Vance (Secondary)*, or *Anita Roy (ASHA)*.
  2. Enter your shift note (e.g., *"Had lunch, took afternoon rest, pleasant mood"*).
  3. Click **"Post Update"**. All caregivers and health workers see this update instantly.

### 4.10 Multi-Channel Messaging with Doctor & ASHA
* Switch between the **🩺 Dr. Thorne** channel and the **🌾 Anita Roy (ASHA)** channel.
* Type a question or clinical update in the input box and click **"Send"**.
* A realistic professional auto-reply arrives within two seconds to demonstrate conversational round-trips.

### 4.11 Configuring Alert Escalation Preferences (Tab 5)
Click **"🔔 Alert Escalation Settings"**:
* **Instant Critical Push Notifications:** Toggle on/off browser notifications for missed high-priority pills.
* **SMS / USSD Fallback (+91 98765 43210):** Ensures alerts deliver even if home Wi-Fi disconnects.
* **Unacknowledged Alert Window:** Set the delay before alerts escalate to Priya (choose 30, 45, or 60 minutes).
* **Batch Routine Digest:** Group non-critical notifications into a morning and evening summary to avoid notification fatigue.

## 5. Clinical & Community Health Hub Guide

The Clinical Hub provides evidence-based decision support for attending physicians, neurologists, and rural field health workers (ASHA/ANM).

### 5.1 Accessing the Clinical Hub (Passcode `9999`)
* On the Space Selection screen, select **Clinical & Community Health**.
* Enter passcode `9999` and click **Log In to Clinical Hub**.

### 5.2 Mandatory Clinical Advisory
All clinical views display a prominent non-diagnostic notice:
> *"Mandatory Clinical Advisory: This platform is an assistive cognitive engagement and routine monitoring decision-support tool. It does not independently diagnose dementia, stage disease severity, or provide clinical treatments. All observations are based on logged activity timelines."*

### 5.3 Reviewing Cognitive Telemetry & Rule Engine Flags (Tab 1)
* **Summary Metric Cards:**
  * **Composite Weekly Score:** 0–100 index (Routine Adherence 40%, Game Performance 40%, Engagement 20%).
  * **Routine Adherence:** Percentage of completed reminders.
  * **Memory Game Accuracy:** Normalized accuracy score and average touch hesitation (in milliseconds).
  * **Active Flags for Review:** Count of pending rule-engine flags.
* **Explainable Behavior Flags:**
  * Every flag (e.g., `routine_latency_streak`, `inactivity_gap`, `game_score_decline`) shows transparent, inspectable evidence directly from raw database records.
  * Clinicians can click **"✓ Mark Reviewed"**, **"✕ Dismiss"**, or **"📝 Add Note"** on each flag.
  * Click **"⚙️ Re-evaluate Rule Engine"** to re-run clinical threshold algorithms against new data.

### 5.4 ASHA & ANM Simplified Mobile Action Report (Tab 2)
Click **"ASHA / ANM Action View"**:
* Optimized for field workers using entry-level mobile devices in rural areas.
* **One-Line Action Summary:** Clear status in green or amber:
  * *"Weekly Status: Adherence 94% • Recommended action: Routine weekly check-in. No emergency home visit required."*
* **Field Checklist:**
  1. Medication Checkpoint (inspect tablet box storage).
  2. Water & Bedside Hydration Check.
  3. Family Language Comfort Check.
* **Home Visit Logging:** Click **"✍️ Log Completed Home Visit Check"** to record an official PHC inspection note.

### 5.5 Generating & Printing the Structured Weekly PDF Report (Tab 3)
Click **"Structured Weekly PDF"**:
* Displays a standardized 7-section clinical report:
  1. Patient & Caregiver Demographics
  2. Non-Diagnostic Medical Advisory
  3. Quantitative Routine Adherence & Task Latency
  4. Cognitive Activity & Engagement Trends
  5. Notable Activity Flags with Explainable Evidence
  6. Qualitative Caregiver Observations (from Sarah Vance)
  7. Mood / Wellbeing Summary & Escalation Incident Log
* **Physician Reply Box:** Dr. Thorne can type clinical recommendations in the reply box and click **"Send Reply to Caregiver"**. The note immediately transmits to Sarah's portal.
* **Print / Save PDF:** Click **"🖨️ Print / Save PDF"** to generate a clean, printer-friendly medical document with all browser navigation stripped away.

### 5.6 Telemedicine Referrals (ABHA) & Compliance Data Bundles
* **Telemedicine Packet:** Click **"📡 Send Telemedicine Referral Packet"** to create a structured referral for district telemedicine or Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) specialists.
* **Data Export:** Click **"📦 Export DPDP/GDPR Data Bundle"** to download an exportable JSON file containing all patient logs, consent records, and telemetry in full compliance with data protection laws.

## 6. Universal Settings & Accessibility Guide

Access the Settings modal by clicking **"Caregiver Settings"** in the navigation bar (protected by PIN `1234`).

### 6.1 Switching Languages & Dialects (5 Regional Locales)
Recollect is fully translated into five regional languages with a focus on Northeast India:
1. **English (`en`)**
2. **Assamese (`as`)** — অসমীয়া
3. **Bengali (`bn`)** — বাংলা
4. **Khasi (`kha`)** — Khasi (Meghalaya)
5. **Hindi (`hi`)** — हिन्दी

* Select your language from the dropdown selector in the top header or within the Settings modal.
* **Universal Localization:** Every button, card, modal, placeholder, calendar day, and weather text instantly updates without reloading the page.
* Your language preference is saved in local browser storage.

### 6.2 Font Scaling (Normal 20pt, Large 24pt, Extra Large 28pt)
* In Caregiver Settings, select the **Patient app text size**:
  * **Normal (20pt):** Exceeds standard web typography baselines for effortless reading.
  * **Large (24pt / +20%):** Recommended for seniors with mild vision challenges.
  * **Extra Large (28pt / +40%):** High-magnification mode for low vision.
* Applies instantly across all senior cards, instructions, buttons, and reminder modals.

### 6.3 Dark Mode (Calm Night Theme)
* Toggle **"Dark Mode (Calm Night Theme)"** in Caregiver Settings (PIN `1234`).
* Applies a soothing deep slate background (`#0b1329` / `#111e3e`) with high-contrast soft text (`#f1f5f9`), engineered to eliminate glare and eye fatigue for seniors in dim or evening lighting.
* When enabled, Dark Mode automatically deactivates High-Contrast mode to ensure clean visual styling.

### 6.4 High-Contrast Stark Theme (WCAG AAA)
* Toggle **"High-contrast theme (Black & White)"** in Caregiver Settings (PIN `1234`).
* Transforms the entire interface into stark black text on pure white backgrounds, with solid 3px borders and no decorative colors, gradients, or shadows.
* Specifically engineered for seniors with low vision or advanced presbyopia, exceeding WCAG 2.1 Level AAA requirements.

### 6.5 Automated Theme & Contrast Scheduling
Caregivers can automate visual modes based on time of day:
1. **Schedule Dark Mode Automatically:**
   * In Caregiver Settings, enable the **"Schedule Dark Mode Automatically"** toggle.
   * Set the **Start Time** (default `20:00` / 8:00 PM) and **End Time** (default `07:00` / 7:00 AM).
   * Recollect evaluates the local clock every 30 seconds. During the configured night window, the tablet automatically shifts into Dark Mode. At dawn, it seamlessly restores day mode.
2. **Schedule High-Contrast Mode Automatically:**
   * Enable the **"Schedule High-Contrast Mode Automatically"** toggle.
   * Set the active daily time window (default `18:00` to `21:00`).
   * During this window, maximum contrast is automatically activated to assist seniors during evening routine checkpoints.
3. **Password Protection:**
   * Because theme and scheduling controls are secured behind the Caregiver PIN (`1234`), seniors living with memory loss cannot accidentally disrupt their visual environment from the bedside tablet.

### 6.6 Low-Power & Reduced Animation Mode
* Toggle **"Low-power mode (Reduced animation)"** in Settings or click the power pill in the header.
* Disables decorative transitions, stops continuous background animation timers, and optimizes battery life for rural tablet usage.

### 6.7 Reviewing the 4-Step Consent & Privacy Framework
Click **"📋 Consent & Privacy"** in Caregiver Settings:
* **Step 1:** Verifies Sarah Vance's power-of-attorney authority.
* **Step 2:** Discloses that all patient data remains stored locally on the tablet.
* **Step 3 (Distinct AI Consent):** Checkbox allowing optional AI pattern analysis. Declining this step *does not disable* Eleanor's reminders, games, or routine logging.
* **Step 4:** Provides plain-language wording to explain the tablet's role to Eleanor.

## 7. Offline Operation & Troubleshooting

### 7.1 Running Without an Internet Connection
* Recollect is an **offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA)**.
* Once loaded, all core features—reminders, pill confirmation, mind games, mood logging, and checklist updates—function 100% locally using `RecollectDB`.
* When an internet connection becomes available, queued actions automatically synchronize with the family caregiver hub.
* You can test offline behavior at any time by toggling the **Online / Offline** status pill in the top header.

### 7.2 Audio & Speech Playback Issues
* **Voice Not Speaking?** Ensure the device media volume is turned up and that browser audio permissions are enabled.
* **Browser Doesn't Support Regional Speech?** Recollect automatically falls back to an integrated Web Audio API pentatonic melodic chime progression so seniors always receive calming acoustic confirmation.

### 7.3 Switching Spaces or Resetting Demo Data
* To switch profiles, click **"Switch Account / Log Out"** in the top navigation bar or click the **"Recollect"** logo.
* If you need to restore all baseline sample data, open your browser's Developer Tools (`F12`), open the **Console** tab, run `localStorage.clear()`, and refresh the page.
